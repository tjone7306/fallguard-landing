Set up in under 2 minutes

No accounts. No hardware. Just a free app and a family code.

1

Install on Apple Watch

The wearer installs FallGuard on their Apple Watch. It starts monitoring automatically in the background.

2

Share the family code

The watch displays a unique code. Text it to a son, daughter, or anyone who should be notified.

3

Family installs the app

Family members install the companion app on their iPhone and enter the code. That's it.

4

Get instant alerts

If a fall is detected, family receives an instant push notification with the time and GPS location.
